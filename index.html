MISSION BRIEF

Overview

NAKSHATRA is an aerospace-focused hackathon organised by the Aerospace Club, Madhav Institute of Technology & Science (MITS), Gwalior. It brings together brilliant minds to develop innovative solutions through two dedicated tracks — Software and Hardware.

  • Team Size: 2 - 4 Members
  • Eligibility: UG, PG, Engg, Mgmt, Arts, Commerce, Law, Medical
  • Inter-college & Inter-branch teams allowed

The Venue

Madhav Institute of Technology and Science (MITS)

Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, India

Final Hackathon Dates

24 - 25 October 2026

THE TRACKS

Choose your trajectory. Each track will feature aerospace-specific problem statements.

Software Track

Develop applications, algorithms, simulations, or AI models to solve complex aerospace and astronomical challenges.

Hardware Track

Design and build physical prototypes, drones, avionics systems, or rovers to tackle real-world engineering problems in aerospace.

PROTOCOLS & GUIDELINES

  • Each participant can be a member of only one team.
  • Teams must select and work on one problem statement only.
  • The submitted solution must be the team's own work. Plagiarism may result in disqualification.
  • Participants must submit their PPT within the specified deadline.
  • Shortlisted teams must participate in the offline Final Round at the MITS campus.
  • The decision of the judging panel will be final.
